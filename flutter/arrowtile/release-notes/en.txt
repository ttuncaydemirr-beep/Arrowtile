Version 1.2.0
- Chapters now have 20 levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Very hard; the board grows from chapter to chapter.
- New long, winding arrows and shaped boards.
- Big boards: drag to move, pinch to zoom, plus a "Fit" button; tap anywhere on an arrow's cells.
- Arrows follow their own path as they leave, with a new wind sound.
- Torch celebration and speed badge at the end of each level.
- Settings: Arrow color (Colorful / Single color).
- Fresh colors and a new font.
